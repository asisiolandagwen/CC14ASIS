PROJECTS

Smart Barangay E-Services Portal

a digital system that automates barangay services such as document requests and concern reporting through a secure web and mobile platform. It integrates Artificial Intelligence (AI) to prioritize and process requests efficiently, blockchain technology to ensure data security and record integrity, and cloud computing to enable online data storage and remote access. The system aims to improve service efficiency, transparency, and convenience for both residents and barangay officials.

Il Padrino: Analysis and Enhancement of the Procurement and Fulfillment Business Process

It focuses on evaluating the current procurement and order fulfillment procedures of Il Padrino to identify inefficiencies and areas for improvement. The project proposes process enhancements and system-based solutions to streamline purchasing, inventory replenishment, and order delivery operations. By improving workflow efficiency and accuracy, the study aims to optimize business performance and customer satisfaction.

Smart Inventory System of Goodshot Cafe

designed to automate the monitoring and management of café supplies, ingredients, and products to ensure accurate stock control and reduce waste. It utilizes digital tracking and real-time updates to help staff monitor inventory levels, prevent stock shortages, and support timely restocking decisions. The system enhances operational efficiency, improves accuracy in inventory records, and supports better business management for the café.
